Our Core Purpose

Healing Through Harmony

Guitars for Vets (G4V) is dedicated to providing physical and emotional relief to combat veterans struggling with post-traumatic stress (PTS) and physical injuries through the healing power of music and community.

01

Acoustic Therapy

Providing structured, one-on-one and group guitar instruction designed to refocus the mind, ease anxiety, and build new cognitive pathways.

02

Lifelong Instruments

Upon graduation of our core program, veterans receive a brand new acoustic guitar and accessories package to sustain their musical journey.

03

Camaraderie & Jam

Creating safe, supportive peer environments via twice-monthly jam sessions at VFW Post #4895 in Clarksville, Tennessee.
